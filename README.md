# Customer Churn Analysis for NexaTel Digital Services Pvt. Ltd.

## 1. Project Overview
Welcome to the NexaTel Customer Churn Analytics project. As a Junior Data Analyst at NexaTel Digital Services Pvt. Ltd., an Indian telecom and digital-services provider, this project simulates a real-world assignment to answer why customers are leaving. 

Working with 757,000 records across 24 interconnected tables, the goal is to clean, explore, and analyze data to show non-technical managers who is churning, the revenue at risk, and actionable steps to retain them.

## 2. Business Context
NexaTel serves customers across India with products like Prepaid/Postpaid Mobile, 4G/5G, Fiber Broadband, and OTT bundles. Monthly churn has risen from 1.9% to 2.7%, while acquisition costs jumped 22%. 

Key questions to answer this quarter:
* Which plans and customer segments have the highest churn?
* Are repeat complaints driving churn?
* Which valuable customers are most at risk?
* Are network black spots causing localized spikes?
* Which states are losing subscribers?
* Are retention campaigns effective?

## 3. Dataset Description
The database consists of 24 CSV files with roughly 757,000 total records, linked via primary and foreign keys.

## 4. Project Tasks & Deliverables
* **Phase 1 (Week 1):** Data Loading, Cleaning & Quality Report (Python/Excel/Pandas | Beginner-Intermediate)
* **Phase 2 (Week 2):** Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) with Charts (Pandas/Matplotlib/Seaborn | Intermediate)
* **Phase 3 (Week 2-3):** Churn KPI Calculations & Insights (Python/SQL/Excel | Intermediate)
* **Phase 4 (Week 3-4):** Dashboard + Management Summary Report (Power BI/Excel/Python | Intermediate)
